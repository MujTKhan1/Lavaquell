    Quick answers

    What is the minimum order quantity?

    MOQ varies by product and contract type. For hotel partnerships, we typically work on monthly volume agreements. Contact us and we will tailor a solution for you.

    Are your bottles certified and safe?

    Yes. AirOWater complies with international and GCC/UAE standards (GSO 1025/2014). Our water passes through multi-stage filtration, ozonation, and careful re-mineralisation. We are HACCP, ISO 22000 & EQM certified.

    Which formats do you offer?

    Still and sparkling water in returnable glass bottles (500ml & 1000ml), single-use glass bottles with custom labelling, aluminium bottles, LAVAQUELL aluminium cans, and Atmospheric Water Generators for the GCC and Africa.
